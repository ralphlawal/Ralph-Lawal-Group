Who We Are

A Group Built for the Modern Era

Ralph Lawal Group was founded with a clear intention — to close the gap between premium creative thinking and real business results. We saw a market where too many brands were underserved by fragmented agencies and one-size-fits-all solutions.

We built a multi-division structure that allows us to serve clients with depth and consistency across every discipline — from brand strategy and media production to software development, education, and consulting.

Today, we operate across Dublin and Nigeria, working with businesses, creators, and organisations that demand quality, clarity, and results.

The Founder

Ralph Lawal

Founder & Creative Director

Ralph Lawal is a product designer, frontend developer, and creative strategist with roots in Dublin and Nigeria. He founded Ralph Lawal Group to build a company that bridges premium creative execution with technology and strategy — across markets, industries, and borders.

With experience spanning brand identity, digital products, media, and education, Ralph leads a multi-disciplinary team committed to delivering work that meets the highest standards.
