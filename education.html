Coding & Digital Education

We teach children, teenagers, and organisations to build with technology — through coding programmes that are practical, engaging, and built for the real world.

Our Programmes

What We Teach & Deliver

From a child's first line of code to full web development courses for organisations — we design and deliver coding education that actually sticks.

👾

Coding for Kids (Ages 7–12)

Block-based and visual coding using Scratch and MIT App Inventor. Children build games, animations, and interactive stories while developing logical thinking from day one.

⌨️

Web Development for Teens (Ages 13–17)

Real code — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Teenagers build and publish actual websites by the end of the programme, with a focus on projects they're proud of.

🐍

Python & Logic Fundamentals

Introduction to Python programming for older learners — variables, loops, functions, and real-world problem solving. Designed to transition students into more advanced study.

🏫

School Coding Programmes

Custom coding curricula designed for primary and secondary schools in Ireland and Nigeria — aligned to national frameworks, delivered in-person or hybrid, with facilitator training included.

🏢

Corporate Digital Skills

Practical coding and digital literacy workshops for teams — from no-code tools and automation basics to web fundamentals for non-developers who need to understand the tech they work with.

🌍

Community & Volunteer Programmes

We run subsidised and volunteer-led coding sessions in underserved communities across Dublin and Nigeria — because access to coding education shouldn't depend on where you're born.

How We Work

Our Delivery Process

01

Needs Assessment

We understand your learners — their age, prior experience, available devices, time constraints, and what success looks like for your organisation or school.

02

Curriculum Design

We map a programme — from weekly session plans to project briefs — built around your learners' level and your desired outcomes. No off-the-shelf templates.

03

Content & Resource Build

Slides, exercises, starter projects, facilitator guides — all produced to a high standard and adapted for in-person or online delivery environments.

04

Delivery & Review

We facilitate with energy, adapt on the fly, and close every programme with an evaluation and outcome report — so you know exactly what was achieved.

What We Teach

Languages & Tools We Cover

Our programmes are built around real, industry-relevant tools — not just toy environments.

Scratch & Block Coding

Perfect for beginners aged 7–12. Develops computational thinking, sequencing, and creative problem solving through visual, drag-and-drop coding environments.

HTML, CSS & JavaScript

The foundation of the web. Learners aged 13+ build and style real web pages, understand how the internet works, and develop skills that open real career doors.

Python

One of the world's most in-demand languages. We teach Python from the ground up — logic, data types, functions, and beginner projects that build genuine confidence.
