Client Overview

About the Client

The client is a growing business operating in a competitive market that required a complete brand overhaul to better reflect their premium positioning and attract a more discerning audience. They approached Ralph Lawal Group after years of inconsistent visual identity that had undercut their perceived value.

The business had strong foundations — an excellent product, a loyal customer base, and a clear sense of what they stood for — but none of that clarity was visible in their brand. The identity they had was generic, unrefined, and disconnected from the calibre of work they delivered.

Our mandate was to create a brand system that looked, felt, and communicated at the same level as the product itself — and to deliver a visual language that could scale across all platforms and materials without losing coherence.

01

The Challenge

The Problem

The client's existing brand communicated nothing distinctive about who they were or what made them different. Their logo was generic, their colour palette arbitrary, and their messaging inconsistent across touchpoints. Potential clients were discounting them before they even reached the conversation stage.

  • No coherent visual identity across platforms and materials
  • Brand positioning did not reflect the premium quality of the product
  • Inconsistent messaging creating confusion in the market
  • Visually indistinguishable from lower-tier competitors
02

Our Approach

The Solution

We built a complete brand identity system from the ground up — beginning with strategy and positioning, moving through visual identity design, and concluding with a full brand guidelines document and asset library ready for immediate application.

  • Strategic brand positioning workshop to define voice and audience
  • Custom logo system with primary, secondary, and icon variants
  • Premium colour palette and typography system with clear usage rules
  • Comprehensive brand guidelines and asset library for internal use

How We Worked

A Structured Process Built for Precision

Every decision in this project was grounded in research and strategic intent. We did not begin designing until we fully understood the client's market, their audience's expectations, and the competitive landscape they were operating in.

The result was a brand that feels inevitable — as though it could not have been any other way. That level of coherence only comes from a process that earns the creative decisions before making them.

01

Discovery & Brand Audit

We reviewed all existing brand materials, interviewed key stakeholders, and mapped the competitive landscape to understand what the brand needed to stand for and stand out from.

02

Strategy & Positioning

We defined the brand's core positioning statement, tone of voice, audience persona, and visual direction — creating a strategic brief that all subsequent design decisions were measured against.

03

Visual Identity Design

Three distinct creative directions were explored and presented. The selected direction was refined through two rounds of client feedback before being locked and extended into the full identity system.

04

Guidelines & Asset Delivery

A comprehensive brand guidelines document was produced alongside a full digital asset library — giving the client everything needed to apply the identity consistently from day one.
